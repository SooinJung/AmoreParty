## 💄 Project Overview

**Amore Pick** is an AI-powered personalized beauty service concept developed through a collaborative project between **Amorepacific and Sookmyung Women's University**.

The project addresses a common challenge in the beauty industry: while consumers have access to extensive beauty-related content, finding products that suit their individual skin conditions, preferences, and budgets remains difficult.

We proposed an integrated beauty platform combining:

- **AI-powered personalized skincare and cosmetics recommendations**
- **Weather- and season-aware product suggestions**
- **Beauty expense tracking and calendar management**
- **An LLM-powered beauty consultation chatbot**
- **Personalized beauty content and review discovery**

Our objective was to explore how Generative AI could improve beauty product discovery while creating a more convenient and personalized customer experience.

![Amore Pick Service Overview](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bb75b45d-75ad-41d4-806a-8f381bc07395)

> **Project Scope:** Amore Pick was developed as a service concept and interactive design prototype. The AI features and business strategies described below were proposed during the planning stage rather than validated through a production deployment.

## 🎯 Problem Definition

### Too Much Beauty Content, Too Little Personalization

Social media platforms such as Instagram, YouTube, and beauty blogs provide an increasing amount of cosmetics-related content.

However, consumers continue to face several challenges:

- **Skin Compatibility:** Popular products may not suit every individual's skin type.
- **Budget Constraints:** Finding suitable cosmetics within a limited budget can be challenging.
- **Individual Differences:** Skin sensitivity, personal color, and environmental conditions affect product suitability.
- **Information Overload:** Comparing reviews and recommendations requires significant time and effort.

![Customer Pain Points](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1052a0cd-6be0-44ca-8032-9c6eb2319f4e)

### Proposed Solution

Amore Pick focuses on personalized recommendations based on individual preferences and contextual information.

**Personalization Inputs**
- Skin type and skin concerns
- Personal color
- Beauty budget
- Weather and seasonal conditions

**Expected Output**
- Personalized skincare and cosmetics recommendations
- Weather-appropriate product suggestions
- Products aligned with individual budgets and preferences

![AI Personalization Concept](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1bb4eca4-aa0b-4e11-9e3c-13d289010549)

### Beyond Product Recommendations

We also proposed a **Beauty Calendar & Wallet** that would help users manage beauty-related purchases and expenses.

By connecting product recommendations with spending management, Amore Pick aims to make beauty care more convenient and financially manageable.

![Beauty Calendar and Wallet Concept](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6213c22a-9aad-45a5-9eb0-fb2ebe3519ab)

## 🔎 Market Research & Competitive Analysis

### Competitive Benchmarking

We researched existing AI-powered beauty platforms, including **Zamface and Beauti** (뷰티풀), to identify opportunities for service differentiation.

Our analysis focused on:

- AI-powered cosmetics recommendations
- Personalization based on skin tone and personal style
- Coverage of skincare, color cosmetics, and hair care
- Consideration of weather and seasonal conditions
- Overall service convenience and usability

### Differentiation Strategy

Based on the competitive analysis, we proposed several differentiating features:

1. Integrated recommendations across skincare, color cosmetics, and hair care
2. Personalization based on skin type, personal color, and budget
3. Weather- and season-aware beauty recommendations
4. Integrated beauty expense tracking
5. Conversational assistance through an AI chatbot
