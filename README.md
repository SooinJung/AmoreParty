## 💄 Project Overview

**Amore Pick** is an AI-powered personalized beauty service concept developed through a collaborative project between **Amorepacific and Sookmyung Women's University**.

The project addresses a common problem in the beauty industry: consumers have access to an overwhelming amount of beauty content, yet often struggle to identify products that suit their individual skin conditions, personal preferences, and budgets.

We proposed an integrated beauty platform combining:

- **AI-based personalized skincare and cosmetics recommendations**
- **Weather- and season-aware product suggestions**
- **Beauty expense tracking and calendar management**
- **An LLM-powered beauty consultation chatbot**
- **Personalized beauty content and review discovery**

Our goal was to design a service that makes beauty product selection more accessible, personalized, and financially manageable.

![Amore Pick Service Overview](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bb75b45d-75ad-41d4-806a-8f381bc07395)

> **Project Scope:** Amore Pick was developed as a service concept, product strategy, and UI/UX prototype. The AI features and business models presented below were proposed as part of the project rather than validated through a production deployment.

## 🎯 Problem Definition

### The Challenge: Too Much Beauty Content, Too Little Personalization

Social media platforms such as Instagram, YouTube, and beauty blogs provide an increasing amount of cosmetics-related content.

However, consumers still face several challenges:

- **Skin Compatibility:** A product recommended by an influencer may not suit another person's skin type.
- **Budget Constraints:** Consumers struggle to find suitable products within their available budgets.
- **Individual Differences:** Skin sensitivity, personal color, and environmental conditions influence product suitability.
- **Information Overload:** Comparing countless reviews and recommendations requires significant time and effort.

![Customer Pain Points](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1052a0cd-6be0-44ca-8032-9c6eb2319f4e)

### Our Proposed Solution

Rather than providing generic beauty recommendations, Amore Pick focuses on personalization using individual preferences and contextual information.

**Personalization Inputs**
- Skin type and skin concerns
- Personal color preferences
- Available beauty budget
- Current weather and seasonal conditions

**Expected Output**
- Personalized skincare and cosmetics suggestions
- Recommendations adapted to weather and seasonal changes
- Products aligned with individual budgets and preferences

![AI Personalization Concept](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1bb4eca4-aa0b-4e11-9e3c-13d289010549)

### Beyond Product Recommendations

We also proposed a **Beauty Calendar & Wallet** to help users manage beauty-related purchases and expenses in one place.

The concept aims to make personalized beauty care more convenient by connecting product selection with spending management.

## 🔎 Market Research & Competitive Analysis

### Competitive Benchmarking

We analyzed existing AI-powered beauty platforms, including **Zamface and Beauti** (뷰티풀), to identify gaps in personalized recommendation services.

Our analysis considered:

- AI-based makeup and color recommendations
- Skin tone and personal style personalization
- Coverage of foundational skincare products
- Adaptation to weather and seasonal conditions
- User experience and product discovery

### Differentiation Strategy

Compared with the services analyzed, Amore Pick proposed a more integrated personalization experience.

**Key Differentiators**
1. Recommendations across **skincare, color cosmetics, and hair care**
2. Personalization based on skin type, personal color, and budget
3. Weather- and season-aware recommendations
4. Integrated beauty expense tracking
5. Conversational assistance through a proposed AI chatbot

- Gender
- Skin type
- Skin concerns
